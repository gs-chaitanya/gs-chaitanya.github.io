I am a **Silicon Engineer (Emulation)** at **Google**, working on platform enablement pipelines and emulation models for next-generation TPUs. I completed my B.Tech in **Electronics & Communication Engineering** at the **Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi**.

Previously, I was a Visiting Student Researcher at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)** under [Prof. Flavien Solt](https://flavien-solt.github.io/) and at **UC Berkeley** under [Prof. Christopher Fletcher](https://cwfletcher.github.io/). My work spans **hardware security & microarchitectural side-channel analysis**, **neuromorphic computing** with ReRAM and FeFET devices, and **open-source EDA flows**. I am a two-time **Google Summer of Code** contributor ([OpenROAD '25](https://theopenroadproject.org/), [Apertium '24](https://www.apertium.org/)) and a **Linux Foundation Mentorship** fellow with RISC-V International.
