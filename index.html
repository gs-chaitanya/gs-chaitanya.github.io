I am a Silicon Engineer (Emulation) at Google, working on platform enablement pipelines and emulation models for next-generation TPUs. I completed my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi.

Previously, I was a Visiting Student Researcher at the National University of Singapore (NUS) under Prof. Flavien Solt and at UC Berkeley under Prof. Christopher Fletcher. My work spans hardware security & microarchitectural side-channel analysis, neuromorphic computing with ReRAM and FeFET devices, and open-source EDA flows. I am a two-time Google Summer of Code contributor (OpenROAD ‘25, Apertium ‘24) and a Linux Foundation Mentorship fellow with RISC-V International.
